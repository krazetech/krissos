Settings and the desktop.

The things you set once, and the keys you'll use every day.

  • Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, printers
  • Main display, Windows+P
  • Taskbar, windows, Start, Mac layout
  • Chinese, Tamil, Jawi
  • On-screen keyboard
  • Screen recording

Connect Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and printers

Open Settings from Start, or press Windows and type settings. For a quick switch, Windows + A opens quick settings with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth toggles.

  1. Wi-Fi

    Settings > Network and internet > Wi-Fi. Pick a network and type its password. Forget removes a saved one.

  2. Bluetooth

    Settings > Bluetooth and devices > Bluetooth > Add a device, then put the headphones, mouse or phone in pairing mode and pick it.

  3. Printers

    KrissOS Welcome > Drivers and extras > Printers adds printing support and a tool to add your printer. Most printers from the last ten years work without a driver. With 110, Settings > Bluetooth and devices > Printers lists the printers on your network by itself, prints a test page, and adds a printer by its address.

The Wi-Fi and Bluetooth pages and the Printers page become KrissOS's own in 110 and are still being tested (#122, #129). Until then, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth open COSMIC Settings' page for the same thing.

Set the main display and use Windows+P

The main display gets the full taskbar, Start, notifications, new windows, the sign-in screen and fullscreen games.

  1. Open Display settings

    Right-click the desktop > Display settings, or Settings > System > Display. Identify shows a big number on each screen.

  2. Arrange

    Drag the screens so they sit like the real ones on your desk. They snap edge to edge.

  3. Pick the main one

    Choose a screen, then Make this my main display. Changes to resolution or refresh rate go back after 15 seconds unless you choose Keep changes.

Windows + P switches between PC screen only, Duplicate, Extend and Second screen only, as on Windows. Use the arrow keys and Enter.

Change the taskbar, windows, Start and icons, or use the Mac layout

Everything here is in Settings > Personalise, and changes straight away.

  • Like Windows or like a Mac: Personalise > Taskbar > Desktop layout picks Taskbar (Start and your apps along the bottom) or Top bar and dock (a menu bar at the top, a dock at the bottom, window buttons on the left). Or right-click the taskbar > Switch to the top bar and dock, and right-click the dock > Switch to the taskbar to go back.
  • Taskbar: alignment, size, combine buttons, hide automatically, tray icons, and the taskbar on all screens.
  • Colours, Background, Lock screen, and Text and contrast for bigger text.

Coming with 110:

  • Taskbar items: search as a box, an icon or hidden, the Widgets button, and taskbar transparency (#128).
  • Personalise > Windows: corners, a border around the window you are using, shadows and animation speed (#130).
  • Personalise > Start: the Start layout (more pins or more recommendations) and Show recently opened files in Start. Personalise > Icons picks an icon pack; the taskbar restarts for a moment to pick it up (#131).

Type in Chinese, Tamil or Jawi

Windows + Space switches to the next language, and Windows + Shift + Space goes back. The taskbar shows which one is on: EN, 拼 (Chinese, Pinyin), த (Tamil) or ج (Jawi). Click it to switch too.

  • Chinese: type the Pinyin, then pick the character.
  • Tamil: type the sound, for example vanakkam for வணக்கம்.
  • Jawi: each letter is on the Latin key that sounds like it.

With 110, Settings > Bluetooth and devices > Keyboard > Languages turns each one on or off (#126, in testing).

Use the on-screen keyboard

On a PC with a touch screen, a keyboard button sits on the taskbar next to the language. Tap it to show or hide the on-screen keyboard. It types in the language that is on, and pressing a key on a real keyboard hides it.

In testing for 110

Record the screen

Windows + Alt + R starts a recording, and the same keys stop it. The first time, KrissOS asks which screen or window to record; it remembers after that. While it records, the taskbar shows a red dot and the time: click it to stop. The video is saved in Videos > Screen Recordings, with sound.

Windows + Shift + S opens a snipping bar: Rectangle, Full screen, or Record the screen. Print Screen still takes a picture of everything.

This part comes with KrissOS 110 and is still being tested (#132).

In testing for 110

Switch user, or let a guest use the PC

Click your picture at the bottom of Start: Change account settings, Lock, Sign out, Switch user and Turn on the guest account.

  • Switch user locks your session and shows the login screen. Your apps stay open; the login screen shows Back to and your name to return.
  • Guest signs in with no password and can't change the system. Its files are emptied every time a guest signs in. Turn off the guest account removes it.
  • To add a person with their own account: Settings > Accounts > Your info and sign-in > Other people.

This part comes with KrissOS 110 and is still being tested (#133).
