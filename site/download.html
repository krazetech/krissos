Before you install

What to expect.

KrissOS is young. The first images are for testing on spare hardware or in a VM, not for your only machine yet.

  • Architecturex86_64 only. Apple Silicon comes later as its own image.
  • DesktopKDE Plasma first. The KrissOS Shell becomes the default at 110.
  • UpdatesRolling, straight from the Arch mirrors plus the [kriss] repo.
  • ReleasesImages are tagged by codename: 110, 115, and on.

Verify

Check the image.

Compare the checksum with the one published on the release page before you write the ISO to a USB stick.

sha256sum krissos-*.iso
