Status as of 25 November 2026, from the KrissOS CI and our own notes.

Virtual machines, every build

Each KrissOS ISO is started and installed by CI before it is published.

  • PC with UEFI (QEMU, OVMF)Boots, installs and starts to the login screen.Works
  • PC with legacy BIOS (QEMU, SeaBIOS)Installs with Limine in BIOS mode, and starts.Works
  • UEFI with Secure Boot on and Microsoft's keys (OVMF)The USB is refused until its key is trusted; installs with Secure Boot on, enrolls the key in MokManager, reaches the login screen.Works
  • ARM64 virtual machine (QEMU, generic image)The aarch64 image boots in CI. Preview only, not offered for download.Works

Graphics

Drivers KrissOS sets up. Tested only where noted.

  • AMD Radeonamdgpu, in the kernel and Mesa.In the kernel, not tested by us
  • Intel graphicsi915 and xe, in the kernel and Mesa.In the kernel, not tested by us
  • NVIDIA GeForce 16 series and newerThe installer adds NVIDIA's open driver (nvidia-open).In the kernel, not tested by us
  • NVIDIA cards older than the GeForce 16 seriesnouveau by default; the NVIDIA 580 driver from Welcome or kriss nvidia setup. On our test laptop, idle power went from about 4.7 W to 6.8 W with it, so the battery lasts about a third less when idle.Works with notes

Macs

  • Intel Macs with the T2 chipThe KrissOS kernel carries the T2 patches (keyboard, trackpad, Touch Bar, SMC).In the kernel, not tested by us
  • Apple Silicon MacsAn Asahi image is built in CI, but nobody has run it on a Mac yet. See the plan.Not yet tested

Other laptops

  • Microsoft SurfaceThe Surface Aggregator driver is in the kernel.In the kernel, not tested by us
  • ASUS laptopsasus-wmi and the ASUS keyboard driver are in the kernel.In the kernel, not tested by us

Add your PC.

Tried KrissOS on your PC? Open a bug report on GitHub and pick Drivers and hardware, or run kriss report (or Settings > System > Report a problem) and say works or what didn't. It adds this PC's parts with your name, Wi-Fi names and serial numbers taken out. We list a PC once we can read the report.
