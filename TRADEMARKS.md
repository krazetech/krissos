# KrissOS trademarks

The KrissOS code is free software under the [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). You can
use it, change it, and share it. The **name and logo** are a separate matter.
They tell people that a download really comes from us, and that's how we keep
users safe from fake or tampered ISOs.

## The marks

The name **KrissOS**, the KrissOS logo, and the "Kriss" names of our components
(such as Kriss Shell and Kriss Welcome) are trademarks of Kraze Tech.

## Things you can do without asking

- Write about KrissOS, review it, make videos about it, or teach it, using the
  name and logo to refer to it.
- Pass on **unmodified** official ISOs and packages, including their checksums
  and signatures.
- Say your project is "based on KrissOS" or "a fork of KrissOS".
- Use the name and logo for community groups, meetups, and fan sites, as long as
  it's clear they aren't run by Kraze Tech.

## Things you need permission for

- Shipping a **modified** ISO, image, or package under the KrissOS name or logo.
  A fork needs its own name and logo. Please also remove our branding, signing
  keys, and update servers from the build.
- Using the name or logo in the name of a product, company, or domain.
- Selling merchandise with the logo.
- Anything that suggests Kraze Tech made, endorses, or supports your project
  when it doesn't.

## Official downloads

Official KrissOS images and packages are signed with the KrissOS signing key
and published only on the KrissOS website and the
[krazetech/krissos](https://github.com/krazetech/krissos) GitHub releases.
Anything from somewhere else isn't from us.

## Other trademarks

KrissOS is based on Arch Linux but isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Arch
Linux. Arch Linux and the Arch logo are trademarks of their respective owners.
The same goes for the other names and logos of upstream projects KrissOS uses.
